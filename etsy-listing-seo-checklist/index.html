What this checklist reviews

The checklist looks for keyword coverage in the title, tags, and description, plus practical listing basics that help shoppers understand the product.

FAQ

Does this guarantee Etsy ranking?

No. It is a practical checklist, not a ranking guarantee. Competition, conversion rate, photos, pricing, reviews, and demand all matter.

Can I use it for POD products?

Yes. It is useful for POD listings, handmade products, and digital downloads.

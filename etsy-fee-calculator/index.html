FAQ

Are the Etsy fee defaults always current?

No. Etsy fees can vary by country and payment setup. Treat the defaults as editable starting points and verify them against Etsy's official fee pages.

Does this include taxes?

No. This calculator focuses on listing economics and does not provide tax, accounting, legal, or financial advice.

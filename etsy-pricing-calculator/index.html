FAQ

Why is the suggested price higher than expected?

Percentage fees apply to buyer-paid revenue, so the listing price must also cover fees on the shipping amount and fixed fees.

Can I use this for POD listings?

Yes. Use your POD base cost as product cost and your provider shipping charge as shipping cost.
